About this map

Positions of landmarks are real, and around the Sun so are the distances: the local view is at true scale. The far galaxies are drawn closer than they are, and say so. Nebulae and clusters are painted rather than measured, and the unnamed ones across the disk stand for the many we do not list. Places from fiction are drawn dashed and violet; where a story names a real star they sit at it, otherwise they are placed by eye. The spiral-arm outlines are fitted to the artwork, which is an impression rather than a survey. {{ galaxy.credits }}

Keyboard: arrow keys move, Shift + arrows turn and tilt, + and − zoom, Home shows the whole galaxy.
